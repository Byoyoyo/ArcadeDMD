English
=======

Sprites used by the scenes (horizontal PNG strip or animated GIF).
All of them were drawn for ArcadeDMD, none comes from a game.

jet.png     generic plane (2 frames) for After Burner II
ring.png    generic ring (6 frames) for Sonic 1 and Sonic 2
tank.png    small generic tank (3 frames of 14x5) for the Metal Slug games
ship.png    small ship (2 frames of 9x5) for Neo Geo shoot'em ups
ball.png    ball (3x3) for Neo Geo sports games
blocks.png  colored blocks (6 frames of 128x5) for Neo Geo puzzle games
disc.png    flying disc (4 frames of 20x3) for Windjammers
car.png     rally car seen from above (2 frames of 10x5) for Neo Geo racing games
runner.png  running fighter (2 frames of 8x10) for Neo Geo action games and beat'em ups
horse.png  galloping race horse and its jockey (2 frames of 14x11) for the Neo Geo horse races
cps_prop.png   propeller fighter of the war (2 frames of 12x6) for 1941, 1944 and 19XX
cps_jet.png    jet fighter (2 frames of 14x5) for Varth, U.N. Squadron, Carrier Air Wing and Giga Wing
cps_flyer.png  flying soldier (2 frames of 12x5) for Forgotten Worlds
cps_steam.png  steam age fighter (2 frames of 13x6) for Progear
cps_dragon.png wizard on his flying beast (2 frames of 13x6) for Dimahoo
cps_eco.png    little pod and its gun arm (2 frames of 11x6) for Eco Fighters
cps_mech.png   mech fighter (2 frames of 12x6) for Mars Matrix
cps_frog.png   frog sitting or leaping (2 frames of 7x5) for Frog Feast
cps_balloon.png  striped hot-air balloon (7x10) for Pokonyan! Balloon
cps_pumpkin.png  pumpkin carriage (2 frames of 9x8) for Magical Pumpkin
cps_sight.png  red sight (7x7) for Ganbare! Marine Kun
fx_pop.png     small explosion (6 frames of 16x16)
fx_fire.png    fireball (8 frames of 32x32) for a big enemy or a bomb
fx_shock.png   lightning and shock waves (8 frames of 32x32) for a bomb or a super shot
fx_star.png    star and debris (6 frames of 32x32) when an enemy is destroyed
fx_plasma.png  blue plasma explosion (5 frames of 32x32) in space and for a super shot

To replace the ring with your own character, drop sonic.gif
(or sonic.png as a strip of 6 frames) into this folder.
For Sonic 2, sonic2.gif (or sonic2.png) takes priority if it exists.
For the Metal Slug games, mslug_tank.gif (or mslug_tank.png as a strip of 3 frames)
replaces the tank. 5 pixels high at most, cannon facing right.
On the HD screen, mslug_tank_hd.png replaces the tank of the first Metal Slug
in the player's card (any size, seen from the side, cannon facing right).
A plain background around the drawing is made transparent, empty margins are cut.
For shoot'em ups, neogeo_ship.gif (or .png) replaces the ship.

For the coin screen of the Neo Geo fighting games (nobody playing),
insert_coin.gif (or insert_coin.png), 128x32, replaces the coin and
INSERT COIN as long as there is no credit. A Pixelcade GIF works.
The coin still drops and the credits still roll when you insert one.

Pour remplacer l anneau par ton propre personnage, depose sonic.gif
(ou sonic.png en bande de 6 images) dans ce dossier.
Pour Sonic 2, sonic2.gif (ou sonic2.png) passe en priorite s il existe.
Pour les Metal Slug, mslug_tank.gif (ou mslug_tank.png en bande de 3 images)
remplace le char. 5 pixels de haut au plus, canon vers la droite.
Sur l ecran HD, mslug_tank_hd.png remplace le char du premier Metal Slug
dans la carte du joueur (toute taille, vu de profil, canon vers la droite).
Un fond uni autour du dessin devient transparent, les marges vides sont coupees.
Pour les shoot'em up, neogeo_ship.gif (ou .png) remplace le vaisseau.

Pour l ecran des pieces des jeux de combat Neo Geo (personne ne joue),
insert_coin.gif (ou insert_coin.png), 128x32, remplace la piece et
INSERT COIN tant qu il n y a pas de credit. Un GIF de la Pixelcade convient.
La piece tombe et les credits roulent toujours quand tu en mets une.

Tes propres animations pour la dalle (un GIF de DOFLinx ou de la Pixelcade
par exemple) vont dans le dossier artwork, a cote d arcadedmd.py. Il n est
jamais publie et une mise a jour n y touche pas.
artwork/boom.gif      explosion d un ennemi, a chaque point marque (shoot'em up)
artwork/boom_big.gif  grosse explosion, pour un gros gain d un coup
artwork/bomb.gif      explosions d une bombe
artwork/shock.gif     onde de choc d une bombe
artwork/super.gif     super tir de Strikers 1945 Plus
artwork/charge.gif    tir charge de Pulstar
Pour un seul jeu, mets-les dans artwork/<jeu>/ (artwork/s1945p/boom.gif par
exemple). Un GIF de 128x32 se joue sur toute la dalle, son noir laisse
voir la scene. Si son dessin tient au milieu (les explosions de DOFLinx),
il est recadre et part a l endroit de l action.
