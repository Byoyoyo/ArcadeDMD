## Capcom CPS1, CPS2 and CPS3 games

Every Capcom CPS1, CPS2 and CPS3 game of FinalBurn Neo is recognized, with MAME as with RetroArch. That makes 88 games and their 942 sets. A version, a bootleg or a hack takes the file of its original game, with its own name when it has one (Street Fighter II' Magic Delta Turbo, for example). A version whose own cheats put a value elsewhere reads it there, in the `"per_game"` section of the file (the second revision of JoJo's Venture, Quiz & Dragons' Japanese resale, the US Eco Fighters). The files are made by `tools/cps_games.py` from the list and the cheat database of FinalBurn Neo, completed by MAME's (the cheat collection of mamecheat.co.uk). MAME gives Street Fighter Zero (CPS Changer) all its values, the credits of Final Fight and Varth, and the characters of JoJo's Venture. RetroAchievements (its public scripts on GitHub and the data `tools/ra_notes.py` downloads) gives the score of 1941, 19XX, Three Wonders, Armored Warriors, Battle Circuit, Cadillacs and Dinosaurs, Capcom Sports Club, Captain Commando, Eco Fighters, Final Fight, JoJo's Bizarre Adventure, Knights of the Round, Mega Man 1 and 2, Mighty! Pang, Night Warriors, Pang! 3, Progear, Quiz & Dragons, Street Fighter Alpha, Street Fighter Alpha 3, Street Fighter II' Hyper Fighting, Street Fighter III 3rd Strike, Strider, Super Gem Fighter, Super Puzzle Fighter II Turbo, The Punisher, U.N. Squadron and Warriors of Fate, and the rounds won of Marvel Super Heroes, Street Fighter Alpha 3 and X-Men vs. Street Fighter, to be checked in game.

![Four Capcom fighting games on their stages, HD display](images/hd_capcom_stages.jpg)

- **What is read.** Each player's health, the timer, the lives, the bombs, the super gauge, the characters and their names, the stage, and the credits when the game keeps them. The timer shows when the cheat gives all of it. In 23 games it only gives a part, one digit, the seconds of a clock or a counter that is not the timer seen in the game. ArcadeDMD then reads it only to see that the game runs, without showing it. The score is not in the cheat databases, learn it with `learn.bat` (Learning the score, below).
- **Who plays.** The Capcom games have no BIOS that tells it. ArcadeDMD watches the timer, the score once it is learned, the health, the lives and the bombs. As long as they move, player 1 plays. After 10 seconds without any change, the coin screen comes back. This needs the timer or the score (or the health in a fighting game), because the lives and the bombs alone change too seldom. Without them, the game stays on its game scene. When the game's credits are read (learned with `learn.bat` if needed), one or two credits spent are a start, and player 1 plays from the select screen on, for 45 seconds until the fight moves.
- **RetroArch.** FinalBurn Neo core. CPS1 and CPS2 share 64 KB of RAM from 0xFF0000, CPS3 its 512 KB from 0x2000000, with its bytes reversed in 32-bit words that ArcadeDMD puts back in order. A value outside of this RAM (the credits of some CPS2 games) is read with MAME only. 1944 and Dimahoo keep all their values outside of it, so RetroArch shows nothing of them and the log says so. Launch these two games with MAME to see everything (in LaunchBox, Edit, then the Emulation tab). Mercs is set for three players in FinalBurn Neo, and with two controllers one of them drives two players. Set its DIP switches to Play Mode 2 Player and Coin Slots 1 (Quick Menu, Core Options, DIP Switches), then restart the game.
- **Credits.** Under RetroArch most Capcom games do not give their credits. Once `coin_buttons.bat` has shown ArcadeDMD your coin buttons, it counts them. A coin gives a credit, and a player who begins takes one. Once the START buttons are shown too, a player who presses START with a credit begins at once. Otherwise the start is seen when the game starts moving, a continue and player 2 joining in when the lives come back from 0 by 2 or more (an extra life is only 1), and in Mercs, which has no lives, when the energy comes back from 0. Player 2 also takes one when his values start moving after 45 seconds without (his score goes up, a life or a bomb spent, a hit taken), out of the fighting games where his health is the computer's. Game files say it in their `"coins"` section. It stays a guess. For an exact count, learn where the game keeps its credits with `learn.bat` (`credits=0`, `credits=1`, `credits=2`...), the game itself then gives them, continues included.
- **Fighting games.** On the panel, the health bars, the timer, the names, and on the bottom line the super gauge of each player, blue as it fills up, gold and blinking when it is full. On the HD screen, the fight scene on the game's own stage, with the place under the stage banner (CHINA, INDIA, FEAST OF THE DAMNED...), and the super gauges under the fighters. Street Fighter II, its Champion Edition and Hyper Fighting, Super Street Fighter II, Street Fighter Alpha and Night Warriors read the rounds won by each player (cheat database), X-Men: Children of the Atom too (found in captures made on a cabinet). They are gold stars under the bars on the HD screen and gold squares next to the timer on the panel, and the winner of a round is the one whose count goes up. Since 1.31.1, Samurai Shodown III to V Special, SNK vs. Capcom, Garou, the three Real Bout Fatal Fury, Art of Fighting 1 and 2 and Kabuki Klash read them too (RetroAchievements code notes, to confirm in game). A health bar that goes below zero at a K.O. (0xFF in its byte, Street Fighter II, the King of Fighters and since 1.31.1 most Neo Geo fighting games, `"zero_from"`) reads empty, it read full and the K.O. was missed. In the King of Fighters '96 to 2002 a fighter at 0 still fights, only below zero is a K.O., so 0 reads 1 (`"add": 1`). Rage of the Dragons and Ragnagard read their health as a signed 16-bit word for that. Fatal Fury, King of the Monsters and Legend of Success Joe keep the computer's fighter in a block of its own, the right bar shows it against the computer and player 2's when he plays. The King of Fighters '94, '96, '98, '99 and 2002 and Real Bout Fatal Fury Special tell their select screens (`select`, RetroAchievements code notes), where VS shows and no event of a fight comes, on the HD screen as on the panel. Both bars emptied at once (the select screen after START, the end of a game) are not a K.O. The super gauge of Marvel vs. Capcom, Marvel Super Heroes, X-Men vs. Street Fighter and X-Men: Children of the Atom has levels, like the game. The bar is the one of the current level (`power`), with POWER and the level beside it (`power_level`, `"max"` for the top level, where the bar shows full with POWER MAX). In Marvel vs. Capcom, Marvel Super Heroes vs. Street Fighter and X-Men vs. Street Fighter each player has two fighters who take turns. The health and the name shown are those of the fighter on screen, made by the engine from the values of both (`"tag"` in the game file, a `"team"` expression), and the team is K.O. only when both are down. Player 2 is a human or the computer. Street Fighter Alpha, Alpha 3, Marvel vs. Capcom, Vampire Hunter 2 and JoJo's Bizarre Adventure read it in the game (cheat database, `"human"` in the `activity` section), and so do Marvel Super Heroes vs. Street Fighter, X-Men vs. Street Fighter and X-Men: Children of the Atom (found in captures made on a cabinet). Player 1 is the computer when player 2 plays alone, read in these games, seen in the others from player 2's START followed by a credit the game spends. In the other fighting games, player 2 is a human from the moment his score goes up (once it is learned, but not in the Marvel games where the computer scores too, `"cpu_scores"` in the `activity` section), he presses START with a credit, or a credit is spent during a fight (a challenger), until nobody plays any more or his score has not moved for two minutes. Street Fighter II reads its credits and the score of player 1, learned with `learn.bat` on a cabinet under RetroArch. Player 2's score is taken at the same place in his own block and is still to be confirmed. A credit spent is then a start, seen at the character select. The fighter of Street Fighter III: New Generation is told by two bytes, a row and a column of the select screen, and the game file joins them (`"join"`).
- **Values read at every frame.** A value marked `"fast"` in a game file is read at every frame (the stick and buttons of The King of Fighters '98, since 1.32). The MAME plugin reads it at each frame, and the RetroArch reader asks for it at each frame between its other reads, which it then asks all at once. Each change keeps the time it was read.
- **The other games.** Each one has its own panel scene and HD scene, see Capcom games done one by one just below.
- `demo_capcom.bat` plays the demo of a Capcom game, most of them with the coin screen first.

## Capcom games done one by one

Like the Neo Geo games, the other Capcom games got a panel scene and an animated HD scene of their own, fifty of them. Everything is drawn by the program, no image, character or logo comes from the games.

- **The games with characters, like Metal Slug (1.30).** The beat'em ups, the action games and platformers, the two Pang and Mercs show no character drawn by the program any more. The place of the game stays behind, calm and darker. Each player has a panel with what is read in the game (gauge, lives, score, bombs, weapon, level), and the middle shows the logo, TIME when the timer is read whole (red when little time is left) and the credits. The game's events show between the panels. Points are a blow in the middle, a punch, a shot, a slash, a thrown weapon, a spell or a balloon that bursts. A bomb or a special leaves the panel and the middle blows up. A life lost turns the panel red and shakes the screen, health that comes back says + LIFE, and a level gained says LEVEL UP! Player 2's panel lights up when his health, his lives or his score move, and goes dark 45 seconds after they stop.
- **Shoot'em ups.** Once the score is learned, an enemy only blows up when the game scores points, as with the Neo Geo. A bomb spent sweeps the screen, the weapon picked up and the plane chosen are named. On the panel, the plane, the ship or the soldier of the game flies and fires. Player 2 comes in when his score goes up, or when he loses a life, spends a bomb or takes a hit, and leaves 45 seconds after they stop. The credits show under the logo. Mercs shows in panels, like the beat'em ups.
- **Action games and platformers.** In their panels, the weapon held is read in Ghouls'n Ghosts and Three Wonders, the armour in Ghouls'n Ghosts, the partner and the floor in Magic Sword, the hero in Willow, and the points of Pang! 3 and Mighty! Pang burst a balloon. Strider and Willow keep one panel, the players take turns.
- **Puzzle games, quizzes and mahjong.** They play by themselves, with our own questions, tiles, gems and bubbles, and follow what is read in the game. A life lost in a quiz is a wrong answer.
- **The small games** (Frog Feast, Pokonyan! Balloon, Magical Pumpkin, Ganbare! Marine Kun) play by themselves too. The frogs leap, the balloon flies over the savanna, the pumpkin carriage rolls through the town, the sight follows the fish.
- Final Fight Anniversary Edition and Final Fight EX (CPS2 hacks) take the scene of Final Fight, Tenchi wo Kurau II CPS Changer the one of Warriors of Fate.
- The values come from the cheat databases of FinalBurn Neo and MAME 2003-Plus and still have to be confirmed in game (`test_retroarch.bat` shows them). The score is in neither of them, learn it with `learn.bat`, the title of the game takes its place on the panel until then. The King of Dragons counts its experience, which is read.
- With nobody playing, the coin screen (see Who plays, just above).
- `demo_capcom.bat` shows them without playing. Pick the game in the list, or give it directly, `demo_capcom.bat ffight` for example.

## Cave games

Every Cave game of FinalBurn Neo is recognized, with MAME as with RetroArch. That makes 37 games and their 158 sets. The games of the Cave board with its 68000 (DonPachi, DoDonPachi, ESP Ra.De., Dangun Feveron, Guwange, Puzzle Uo Poko), the games Cave made for the IGS PGM (DoDonPachi Dai-Ou-Jou, Ketsui, Espgaluda, and DoDonPachi II that IGS made under Cave's licence), the games of Cave's own CV1000 board with its SH-3 (Mushihime-sama, Espgaluda II, Ibara, Pink Sweets, Muchi Muchi Pork!, Deathsmiles, DoDonPachi Dai-Fukkatsu, SaiDaiOuJou, Akai Katana...), and the games other companies made on the Cave board (Power Instinct 2, Sailor Moon, Air Gallet, Hotdog Storm, Mazinger Z...). A version, a bootleg or a hack takes the file of its original game. Progear, made by Cave for Capcom's CPS2, is with the Capcom games. The files are made by `tools/cave_games.py` from the list and the cheat database of FinalBurn Neo, completed by MAME's (the cheat collection of mamecheat.co.uk), which knows more for many games.

![DoDonPachi on an LCD marquee, HD display, KAKOU, the boss of stage 3, and its life](images/hd_dodonpachi.jpg)

- **What is read.** The credits, the lives, the bombs and in DoDonPachi and Dai-Ou-Jou their slots (three at the start, one more for each ship lost, six at most), the shot level, the combo gauge that drains, the ship chosen (TYPE-A, TYPE-B, TYPE-C), the stage or the round, the hero of Guwange and his energy, the multiplier of ESP Ra.De., the spirit stones of Espgaluda, the energy of Deathsmiles, the loop of DonPachi and DoDonPachi (cheat databases of FinalBurn Neo and MAME). The fighting games read the health of each player and the timer, the beat'em ups the health and the lives.
- **What to learn.** The score and the hit counter are not in the cheat databases. Learn them with `learn.bat` (Learning the score, below), `score=` and `score2=`, `hits=` for the number of the HIT counter, `stage=` for the stage. DoDonPachi's are already learned on a cabinet, the score of each player with the continues used as its last digit as the game shows it, the hit counter of each player, the credits. The CV1000 games read their credits, lives and bombs from MAME's cheats, their score is still to learn. RetroAchievements gives the score of DonPachi, ESP Ra.De., Dangun Feveron (the sum of its stages), Puzzle Uo Poko, Gaia Crusaders, Power Instinct 2, Gogetsuji Legends, Sailor Moon, Ketsui, Akai Katana, Deathsmiles and its MegaBlack Label, and Puzzle! Mushihime-Tama, to be checked in game.
- **Versions.** A version whose own cheats put its values elsewhere reads them there, each value where the same cheat of that version writes (Dai-Ou-Jou V100 and its Black Label, Guwange Special, the first versions of DoDonPachi II), in the `"per_game"` section of the file. A value its cheats do not give is left to learn rather than read at a wrong place. The world version of Dai-Ou-Jou (DoDonPachi III) has its own cheats in MAME, its credits, lives and bombs are read there and its shot level and combo gauge are left to learn.
- **RetroArch.** FinalBurn Neo core. The 64 KB of the 68000's work RAM of the Cave board, from 0x100000 (0x200000 in Guwange, 0x300000 in Hotdog Storm and Koro Koro Quest, 0x340000 in Crusher Makochan, 0x400000 in Power Instinct 2 and Gogetsuji Legends), the 128 KB of the PGM from 0x800000, and the CV1000's RAM from 0xC000000, as far as the values of the game go (2 MB for most of them, up to 6 MB for Muchi Muchi Pork! and Dai-Fukkatsu). The bytes are swapped in 16-bit words, and ArcadeDMD puts them back in order. Metamoqester keeps its values out of this RAM and is read with MAME only.
- **Credits and who plays.** Like the Capcom games. A game whose credits are not read counts them from the coin buttons (`coin_buttons.bat`). Once the score is read, player 1 plays while it moves, and the coin screen comes back 10 seconds after it stops. Until then the game stays on its game scene. DoDonPachi says it itself, its game state gives player 1 alone, player 2 alone, both, a continue or the ship select, and each player's ships tell who came in (1.31).
